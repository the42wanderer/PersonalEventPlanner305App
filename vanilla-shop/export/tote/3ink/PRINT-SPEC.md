# Tote bag, The Vanilla Shop Negombo: Ceylon stamp, three colours (front)

This replaces the seven-colour version in `../`. Same bag, same size, three screens instead of seven.

Bag: natural cotton canvas, 15 × 16 in (381 × 406 mm). Art: 270 × 337 mm, centred, 48 mm below the top seam.

## Files

- `vanilla-tote-stamp-3ink_SEPARATIONS.svg`: vector master, one layer per ink (named with ink, Pantone and print
  order), plus a canvas-colour preview layer marked "do not print". All lettering is outlined.
- `vanilla-tote-stamp-3ink_FILMS.pdf`: three pages, one positive per screen, black on white at 100%, with
  registration marks and a label on each page.
- `vanilla-tote-stamp-3ink_preview.png`: flat artwork. `_mockup.jpg` and `_mockup-detail.jpg`: the three films on
  canvas, for approval only.

## Inks (print in this order)

| # | Ink | Pantone (suggested) | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marigold | 123 C | Sky, flower petals, pod tips, skin and house walls |
| 2 | Vermilion | 7417 C | Stamp frame; CEYLON, ලංකා, இலங்கை and 25c are knocked out of it |
| 3 | Forest green | 7743 C | All line work and hatching, the vine, the perforated outline, the caption, the Latin name, the postmark with the logo |

Unprinted canvas is the stamp's paper, the clothes and the light parts of the flowers. Ask for water-based ink for
the soft, printed look in the mock-up; plastisol will come out brighter and crisper.

## Notes for the printer

- Inks 1 and 2 spread about 0.17 mm under the green (trapping), so the films overlap on purpose. Green prints last
  and is not spread.
- The leaves and pods carry a diagonal green hatch tint (about 0.9 mm pitch); keep it as lines, not a halftone.
- Smallest details are about 0.35 mm, so use a fine mesh (about 120–160 T, or 305–355 in US threads) for the green.
- The thin green outline round the perforated edge is what shows the stamp's shape on the canvas: keep it.
- Proof one bag on the actual canvas first: the fine hatching on the leaves and pods has to survive the print.

## Lettering

Top band: CEYLON with ලංකා and இலங்கை stacked beside it. Left strip: VANILLA PLANIFOLIA. Value: 25c. Caption:
THE VANILLA SHOP / NEGOMBO (NEGOMBO about 6 mm tall). Postmark, top left: THE VANILLA SHOP / NEGOMBO · SRI LANKA round the logo. Set in
Gloock, Geist, Noto Serif Sinhala and Noto Serif Tamil (all OFL). Have a native speaker check the Sinhala and
Tamil before films are made.
