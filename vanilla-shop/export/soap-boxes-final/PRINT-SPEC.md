# Soap boxes: final print files (Bloom duo)

Four boxes on the current soap box's flat layout (`Soap__packs.pdf`): 190.3 × 221.5 mm flat, 3 mm bleed.
The front (135.7 × 81.2 mm) sits in the middle of the wrap, with four walls round it. The back panel is joined above the
top wall and is printed upside down, so it reads correctly once folded.

| Soap | Name on the box | Files |

| Rosette Soap | Vanilla & Rosette | `rosette_box_*` |
| Charcoal Soap | Vanilla & Charcoal+ (was Ebony & Amber) | `charcoal_box_*` |

## Files

| Folder | Use |
|---|---|
| `print/` | CMYK artwork with all text live and fonts embedded. The areas to be foiled are left unprinted, as paper. |
| `foil/` | Foil plates: solid black = gold foil. One per box, in register with the print file. |
| `proof/` | For checking only. Magenta = cut; cyan dashes = folds; the foil is simulated; pink = text that needs checking. |
| `fronts.png`, `mockup-boxes.png`, `all-four-proofs.jpg` | Previews. |

**Foil:** the word "Vanilla" on the front, and again on the right wall, in one gold foil. Run a physical foil proof on the
real board before the full run, especially gold on the honey Vanilla box.

**Die:** the PDF this came from has no separate die line, so the cut and folds in the proofs are taken from the
artwork's edges. Check them against the printer's die before films.

## Before print

1. **Charcoal and Aloe:** supply the real barcodes, MFD/EXP and TFM.
2. **Net weight:** the boxes say 100 g; the website says 114 g. Confirm which is right.
3. **Ingredients are incomplete on every box** (they stop at "glycerin,").
   - Aloe Vera's box lists aloe vera.
   - "Charcoal" and "rose extract" are added from the website's ingredient list; they are not on the current boxes.
   - Production should supply the full list for each soap.
4. **The +:** decide what it stands for. If it means something (more charcoal, an added ingredient), add one line to the back.
5. **"Grown on our own farms":** confirm this is true for every bar.
6. **Sinhala and Tamil:** a native speaker checks them (the Tamil is marked pink on the proofs).
7. **Claims:** "reduces fine lines" / anti-ageing should be checked against the NMRA cosmetic rules.
8. **Dates:** MFD/EXP are better stamped per batch than printed.
