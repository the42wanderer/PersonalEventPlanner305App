## Observations
- At least six unrelated visual styles: white/gold bottle labels, yellow floral soap boxes,
  coloured cloth bags, kraft pouches, a green tea box, a black floral hamper.
- The website itself is coherent: espresso brown #1E1511, cream #FBF6EC, gold #C6A668,
  Playfair Display (with italics) + Lato. This is the brand's de facto palette, not forest green.
- Butterfly-pea soap exists as "Nil katarolu" in the Hand Made Soap line.
- Vanilla powder is listed as "Vanilla Tea Powder 50g" in the Vanilla Powder category —
  the name and the 100 g figure in internal notes disagree.
- Candles are not listed on the website.
- Ingredient lists on the site are generic ("Black or Green", "e.g. Gum Acacia or Corn Syrup")
  and cannot be used on labels as written.
- Site claims (Lab verified, Award winning, Organic, Fair trade, "No synthetic vanillin — ever",
  "Sri Lanka's first") need evidence before they appear on packaging.
