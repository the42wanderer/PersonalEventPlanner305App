# Christmas hamper lid

Rigid lid-and-base gift box, 365 × 227 × 105 mm (from the box maker's sample). Holds a wine bottle, a 500 g Christmas cake
and a candle in its new box.

**Design:** one gold foil on a deep matte board, in green or red. The vanilla line art is our own:
- a vine garland with hanging pods across the top;
- the logo and "A Vanilla Christmas";
- a row of Christmas trees drawn as vanilla vines, each with a vanilla flower for its star.

## For the box maker

- **Lid depth and wrap:** the files cover the lid top only. Ask the maker for:
  - the lid depth;
  - the wrap's turn-in.

  Then the walls go in the plain ground colour, with no artwork. The base is plain, in a deeper shade of the same colour.
- **Colours:**
  - green #1E3B2A;
  - red #7A1C22;
  - match on the actual matte paper, or use a coloured paper stock and skip printing the ground altogether. The samples
    look like coloured paper with foil only.
- **Lines:** the finest foil line is 0.28 mm. Check it's within the maker's foil limit. If not, thicken the back row of
  trees to 0.35 mm.
- **Inside:** fit an insert tray so the bottle (about 32 × 8 cm), the cake and the 9 cm candle box can't move.
  Check the internal height is at least 10 cm for the candle box standing upright.
