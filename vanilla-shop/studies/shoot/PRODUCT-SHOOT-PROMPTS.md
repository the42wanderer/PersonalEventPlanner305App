# The Vanilla Shop: product shoot prompts for GPT image

Four reference boards (attach all four, in this order; one slot is left for a fifth image if needed):

1. `1-tea-boxes.jpg`: Natural Vanilla Tea, 12-bag cube (88×85×85 mm) and 25-bag long box (176×85×85 mm), 3/4 views
   and flat panels.
2. `2-pouches.jpg`: 100 g kraft stand-up pouches with the arched paper labels (Natural Vanilla, Vanilla & Cinnamon).
3. `3-candles.jpg`: four candle boxes (90×90×95 mm: Aura navy, Zest ochre, Velvet crimson, Harmony green) and the
   matching coloured glass jars with 60 mm labels.
4. `4-tote.jpg`: the canvas tote, stamp front and logo back.

How to get good results:
- Generate one scene per chat turn. Ask for the lineup first, then the single-product shots.
- Image models redraw packaging. Keep the prompt insistent that designs are copied exactly. Small text will drift,
  so judge the shot on shapes, colours and big type, and use real product photos where fine print matters.
- If a product comes out wrong, reply with "Fix only the [product]: match reference [n] exactly" rather than
  regenerating everything.
- Optional fifth image: a photo of the real shop counter or a table surface you want used.

## Shared block (paste at the start of every prompt)

The attached boards show the real Vanilla Shop packaging. Reproduce every product exactly as designed: same
proportions, colours, illustrations, typography and layout, with the large lettering correct ("Vanilla Tea",
"Vanilla & Cinnamon Tea", "AURA", "ZEST", "VELVET", "HARMONY", "CEYLON", "THE VANILLA SHOP"). Do not redesign,
simplify, add or invent any text, logos or products. Real physical materials: matt paper board with crisp folded
edges and a slight grain, kraft paper pouches with soft creases, coloured glass jars with real reflections and
cream wax, natural cotton canvas with visible weave. Photographed, not rendered: a real camera, real light, true
scale between products, natural contact shadows. No floating objects, no CGI gloss, no plastic sheen, no props
with text.

## Checks before using a result

- Names spelled right and on the right product (AURA navy, ZEST ochre, VELVET crimson, HARMONY green).

- Tea boxes: "Vanilla Tea" in the italic serif; cube 12 bags, long box 25 bags.
- No extra products, fake text, extra logos or warped boxes.
