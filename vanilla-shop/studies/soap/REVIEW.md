- the website product page `thevanillashop.lk/products/vanilla-bathing-bar`, its product data, and the photos of all

**Pack:** a box with chamfered corners. The flat artwork is about 190 × 140 mm: the front with four side walls round
it. The back panel, about 136 × 81 mm, is a separate piece. So the box is roughly 136 × 81 × 28 mm. This is an
estimate from the PDF; there is no die line in it.

## Errors on the current boxes

**Copy (back panel, identical on Vanilla and Rosette):**
1. The headline ends in mid-air: "…with the Pure Vanilla Bathing Bar by" and then nothing. The first body line
   repeats it, again ending in "by".
2. Spelling and grammar:
   - "fine line" should be "fine lines";
   - "benifits" should be "benefits";
   - "its warm aroma", "our premium…" and "choose the vanilla shop…" start sentences in lower case;
   - "care for skin  spirit" is missing "&" and has a double space;
   - "The Vanilla shop", "the vanilla shop" and "The Vanilla Shop" are all used.
3. **The Rosette back is word for word the Vanilla one.** It never mentions rose. The Aloe box (from the photo)
   has its own copy, so Rosette and presumably Ebony & Amber don't.
4. The side panel says "**Import** Notice"; it should be "Important Notice". There is also a stray space in
   "only .".
5. The badge says "Made exclusively with Our own farmed vanilla", with a capital O mid-sentence.

**Ingredients:**
6. The box lists: "Vanilla Planifolia: Bean Extract / Vanilla Planifolia: Bean Powder / Sodium Palmate, Sodium Palm
   Kernelate, Glycerin,". It ends in a trailing comma and is clearly incomplete: no water, no fragrance or essential
   oil, and **no rose on the Rosette box**.
7. **The website lists different ingredients** (coconut, palm, olive "or" shea butter; "essential oils (optional,
   depending on variant)"). Neither list can go on a pack as it stands. Production needs to supply one INCI list per
   variant, in descending order.

**Facts that disagree:**
8. The box says net weight **100 g**; the website says **114 g** for all four.
9. **Maker details differ from the extract labels.** The soap says No 48, Sir Marcus Fernando Mawatha, Colombo 07,
   hotline +94 11 218 7287 and +94 11 287 8766. The extracts say Level 26, World Trade Center, with
   +94 11 287 8766, +94 70 513 0907 and +94 11 743 0250. Confirm which address is current.
10. The product names differ: the website says "Rosette Soap"; the box says "Vanilla & Rosette".

**Languages and print:**
11. **The Tamil is broken.** Vowel signs have come apart: dotted circles appear in தோல் ("skin"), and the opening
    ெ is missing from "வெளிப்புற" on the side panel. This happens when Tamil is typeset in Illustrator without the
    complex-script composer. It needs re-setting and a native speaker's check. The Sinhala looks intact, but get it
    checked too.
12. MFD and EXP are printed into the artwork (Vanilla 01/09/2025 to 30/08/2027; Rosette 02/04/2025 to
    01/04/2027). There is no batch number. Dates and batch should be stamped per run.
13. **Claims.** "Reduces fine lines", "anti-aging" and "100% natural" alongside sodium palmate: anti-ageing claims
    on soap may count as a medicinal claim under Sri Lanka's NMRA cosmetic rules. Worth checking before reprinting.

**Design:**
14. The front art is a collage of vintage botanical cut-outs: passionflowers, a beetle, a bird, a garden bench,
    lilies. There is no vanilla in it except the logo. The pieces look like stock illustration, so confirm the
    licence. It shares nothing with the new extract labels or the tea boxes.
15. All four use the same yellow collage, with only the badge colour changing. On a shelf they read as one product.

## What's already right

- TFM (total fatty matter) is stated: 70% Vanilla, 63% Rosette.
- The usage warning is in three languages.
- Both barcodes are valid EAN-13s.
- The MRPs match the website.
