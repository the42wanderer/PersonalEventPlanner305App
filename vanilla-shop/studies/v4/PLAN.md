# Vanilla Tea — v4 plan

## Brief
1. Anyone sees vanilla and tea instantly.
2. Reads as the current wave of tea/matcha brands, not heritage.
3. Nothing to decode.
4. Built on the café's own colours: espresso #1E1511, cream #FBF6EC, gold #C6A668.
5. Stretches to the rest of the range.

Type (draft): Gambarino for the name, General Sans for labels (both Fontshare, free commercial use).
Front panel: 87.66 × 84.74 mm. Wireframes: wireframes.png (hatched boxes = assets to generate).

## 1. Aroma
What makes vanilla tea different from tea is the scent, so the box shows the aroma: a fine grain
plume (amber at the cup, honey higher up) rising out of a cup of amber tea, with one real cured pod
resting across the rim. Same visual language as the matcha-set reference (substance drawn as grain).
Code-made and final: plume, cup, type. To generate: the pod photo.
Range: same plume over each product's hero object (bottle, jar, soap bar); colour per product.

## 2. Object on colour
The real ingredients shown like jewellery: a cured pod and a fresh tea sprig crossed, oversized,
in hard daylight with a crisp shadow, on a flat tea-green field. Range: each product's real hero
object on its own colour.

## 3. Shade garden
Vanilla is a shade-grown vine. The box carries late-afternoon light through vanilla leaves, with a
glass of amber tea and a pod in the light. The photo wraps the front and the brewing side.
Same visual language as the Komorebi reference.
