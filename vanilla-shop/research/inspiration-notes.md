# Inspiration notes (15 Oct 2026)

References: matcha set (halftone), Komorebi, Yoko, "Green Tea" (AI mock-up, gibberish copy),
Botanik, Godcha Matcha Cafe (Behance 250462167), Matcha box (Behance 225747191),
Dose of Island (doseofisland.com).

## What the strongest ones do (the reasoning, not the features)
- Matcha set: draws the substance, not the plant. The halftone dots are powder; the shape is a bowl
  or a rising sun. One ink; each box in the set is a different crop of the same gesture.
- Komorebi: the name is the image. "Sunlight through leaves" is printed as dappled shadow across
  the whole box; the wordmark wraps the corner so the box reads as an object, not a front panel.
- Botanik: an old engraving made contemporary by an un-botanical ground colour (pink).
  Heritage drawing + heritage colours reads old; heritage drawing + a surprising colour reads new.
- Yoko / Godcha: personality comes from one character, not from decoration. Godcha is a cafe
  brand, like The Vanilla Shop; the packaging is the cafe's signature, the character carries it.
- Matcha pairs green with pink because they sit opposite on the colour wheel: maximum life
  without black. The lesson is "find your hero colour's partner", not "use pink".
- Dose of Island: the can performs the claim. A white can, a wordmark, one coloured hairline per
  flavour and three numbers says "clean label" before you read anything; clutter would contradict
  it. The expressive layer (saturated colour fields, sunlight, fruit) lives in photography, not on
  the can. The range scales by changing one line colour.

## Applied to The Vanilla Shop (hypothesis to test)
- The claim to perform is "real vanilla, not synthetic vanillin". The visible proof of real
  vanilla is the seed specks. A stipple/speck field (vanilla caviar) on cream, forming one bold
  shape per product, could do for vanilla what the halftone does for matcha.
- Brand world already exists: espresso #1E1511, cream #FBF6EC, gold #C6A668, italic serif
  (website, shop sign). Packaging = quiet signature; the expressive layer = the café and the Reels.

## Fonts
- Fontshare (ITF Free Font License): commercial use incl. print, packaging, logos, free.
  Licensee must download from fontshare.com; files may not be passed to designers or printers
  (outlined artwork may be). Shortlist: Gambarino, Zodiak, Erode, Recia, Rowan, Sentient,
  Gambetta (serif); General Sans, Satoshi, Switzer (sans). Specimen: type-specimen-fontshare.png
- Adobe Fonts (with a Creative Cloud plan): licensed for print and packaging; activate in CC,
  outline for the printer. Candidates to try: Ivy Presto / Ivy Mode, Freight Big Pro,
  Bely Display, Orpheus Pro, Roc Grotesk.
