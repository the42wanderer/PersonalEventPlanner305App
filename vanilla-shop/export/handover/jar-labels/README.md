# The Vanilla Shop: jar labels, No. 04 Vanilla Paste and No. 05 Vanilla Powder (100 g)

On the printer's template: 200 × 42 mm wrap, 3 mm bleed. Same design as the extract bottle labels: the colour half
with the painted vanilla flower, the brand line, story and facts; the cream half with the front card. The two new
colours join the range: **cocoa** for the paste (No. 04) and **plum** for the powder (No. 05).

**Languages** (Sri Lanka's labelling regulations, from 1 July 2026):
- **Front card:** the name in English and Sinhala (වැනිලා පේස්ට් / වැනිලා කුඩු).
- **Right of the card:** the name in Tamil (வெனிலா பேஸ்ட் / வெனிலா தூள்).
- **Sizes:** all names bold, with letters 3 mm high.
- **Dates:** MFD and EXP are labelled in all three languages.

## In the masters

**Layers:**
- **`Artwork`:** everything that prints.
- **`TO_FILL`:** pink boxes over everything not yet confirmed. Delete each once filled.
- **`Sinhala_Tamil_live_text`:** hidden. Editable copies of the Sinhala and Tamil, which print as shaped outlines.
  To change one, show the layer, turn on Paragraph > World-Ready Paragraph Composer, edit or paste from the copy
  sheet, then delete the outline.
- **`Dieline_do_not_print`:** the cut line.

**English text:** live, one text object per line. Centred lines are placed by their left edge, so they open in
place; re-centre with the Align panel after editing.

**Painting:** an embedded image.
