# Tote bag, direction A: මීගමුව (Negombo), in the Arugam Bay style

Attach `negombo-ai-references.jpg` (numbered 1 to 9). Reference 1 is the style; 2 to 9 are Negombo.

How the Arugam Bay pin works, and what we keep:
- Two inks on off-white: vermilion and green. The Sinhala name is the hero, in a boxed panel at the top.
- A ruled frame with strict symmetry at the top (number discs, palms, small animals), broken by one huge
  line-drawn subject that runs off the frame (the fish).
- Solid shapes mixed with open line drawings; flat folk drawing; a slightly odd centre (the sun with eyes).
- Insider details only someone who has been there knows: the "98 PANAMA" bus board, cows on the road, bicycles.
- Lorry-art ray fans in the bottom corners, stars flanking the titles.

Negombo's versions: the oruwa sail is the huge subject; the vanilla flower is the sun; planes coming in low over
the beach (the airport town); the red tuk-tuk; St Mary's twin towers ("Little Rome"); fish laid out on the sand.
The boards carry the airport bus (route 187 to Katunayake, to be checked).

The AI draws pictures only. All lettering (Sinhala, Tamil, English, numbers) is set afterwards in vector, so
every text area is left empty.

## Prompt 1: art side

Using the attached reference board, create a poster-style illustration for a screen-printed tote bag, in exactly
the style of reference 1: a two-colour folk travel print, flat vermilion red (#E0452B) and jade green (#1E7A4F) on
a plain off-white ground, portrait format 4:5. Bold, slightly naive hand-drawn linework like a linocut or
screen print, mixing solid filled shapes with open line drawings. No gradients, no shading, no texture, no
speckle, no letters, numbers or words anywhere.

Layout, following reference 1:
- Top: a wide rectangular box with a thin green border, left completely empty inside, with a small red
  eight-point star at each end.
- Below it, a tall panel framed by a thin green rule, containing the scene.
- Top of the panel, symmetrical: in each upper corner a solid red circle left empty inside, with an empty space
  beneath it; next to each, a coconut palm in red; above each palm a small passenger plane coming in low, drawn in
  red line (reference 8).
- Centre top: a large vanilla orchid flower seen face-on, drawn as the sun: green, with long pointed petals and
  thin rays radiating all round it like reference 1's sun, its centre a rolled trumpet-shaped lip (references 3
  and 4), with two small almond-shaped eyes in the centre like reference 1's sun. A small solid red sun disc sits
  just below it.
- Below the flower, small and in green line: the twin towers of a cream colonial church facade (reference 5)
  standing on the horizon.
- The big subject: an outrigger canoe with a huge curved square sail (references 2 and 9), drawn in red line with
  the sail's stitched panels and the outrigger float, so large that the sail runs off the right edge of the
  frame, like reference 1's fish.
- Left of the canoe: a red three-wheeler tuk-tuk seen from the front (reference 7), in red line like reference 1's
  bicycle. Below it, five rows of wavy green lines for the lagoon.
- Lower centre: a small row of fish laid out on the sand, solid red (reference 6), in the place of reference 1's
  cow.
- Bottom: a wide empty area for a title, flanked by two green eight-point stars, with a red ray fan of thick
  radiating stripes in each bottom corner, like reference 1.

Keep every element large, clear and evenly spaced, as in reference 1, with nothing smaller than a thumb at tote
size. The image will be separated into two spot colours for screen printing, so use only the two inks and the
off-white ground.

## Prompt 2: brand side

Using reference 1 for style only, create a matching second panel in the same two inks (vermilion #E0452B and jade
green #1E7A4F on off-white), portrait 4:5, same naive linocut line, no text, numbers or letters anywhere, no
texture.

- A thin green rule frames the whole panel, with a smaller empty box at the top and a red eight-point star at each
  end, mirroring the art side.
- Centre: a bundle of long cured vanilla pods tied in the middle with a string bow, drawn large in red line with
  fine lengthwise wrinkles, set in front of a green sunburst of thick radiating rays.
- Around the bundle, a ring of four small motifs in green line: a teacup, a cinnamon quill, a vanilla flower and a
  small sail.
- Below: two wide empty bands for lettering, separated by a row of small green diamonds.
- Bottom corners: red ray fans, as on the art side.
