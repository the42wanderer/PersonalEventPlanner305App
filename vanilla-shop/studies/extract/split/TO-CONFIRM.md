## 2. Checks I'd like done

- The Sri Lanka Food (Labelling and Advertising) Regulations: whether the shops (Glomark, Kapruka) or the Food
  Control Administration Unit require Sinhala and Tamil on the label. The current label is English only; if needed,
  I'll fit them into the back panels.
- The history line has been checked against sources (botanic gardens, 19th century; hand pollination). Keep it as is
  unless you have a firmer source.

## 3. Still to design

- **100 ml and 200 ml.** The mock-ups scale the 500 ml label down, which makes the back text too small. I need the
  real label sizes for those bottles (or the bottles' diameter and label height) to lay them out properly.
- **Neck strip.** Restyle it to match the new labels.
- **Production files.** Once the facts are in: vector PDFs with all type outlined, 3 mm bleed, a separate die-cut
  line, CMYK colour, and the artwork at 300 dpi or more. I also need the printer's spec: label stock (paper or film),
  finish (matt, uncoated, varnish) and whether they print white ink.
