Vintage postage stamp for a tote bag, portrait 4:5, printed in ONE ink: black (#1A1714) on warm off-white paper (#F1E9D6). A 1930s Ceylon recess-engraved stamp: every tone is engraved parallel lines and cross-hatching that follow each form, closer for shadow, merging into solid black; bare paper is the only white. No grey, colour, gradients, halftone dots or soft shading. Bold, clean lines for screen printing at 27 cm wide: no hairlines or specks. Slightly uneven ink, like a real print.
