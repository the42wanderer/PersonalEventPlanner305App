# Soap boxes: Vanilla Bathing Bar and Vanilla & Rosette (Bloom design)

## The box

Built on the current box's own flat layout, from `Soap__packs.pdf`:
- **The main wrap:** 190.3 × 140.2 mm. The front (135.7 × 81.2 mm) with four walls round it.
- **The back panel:** 135.7 × 81.3 mm, joined above the top wall and printed upside down so it reads correctly once
  folded.

The PDF has no separate die line. The cut outline and folds in the PROOF files are taken from the artwork's edges,
so **check them against the printer's die** before films.

## Before print

1. **A native speaker checks the Sinhala and Tamil.**
   - The Tamil had broken characters on the old boxes and was rebuilt from them.
   - Two Tamil lines were also reworded, so they are marked pink on the proof: the warning and "bathing bar"
     (பாத்திங் பார்).
2. **The ingredients are copied from the old box, and are incomplete.**
   - The list has no water, fragrance or (for Rosette) rose.
   - Production should supply the full list for each variant.
3. **Net weight:** the box says 100 g; the website says 114 g. Confirm which is right.
4. **Address:** the soap boxes and the extract labels give different addresses and phone numbers. Confirm which is
   current.
5. **MFD and EXP** are printed as on the old boxes. Better to stamp them per batch.
6. **The anti-ageing / "reduces fine lines" claim:** check it against the NMRA cosmetic rules.
