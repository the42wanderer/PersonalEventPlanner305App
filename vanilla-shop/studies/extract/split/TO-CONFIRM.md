# Extract labels: what's left before print

All seven wraps are designed:
- No. 01 and No. 02 at 500, 200 and 100 ml;
- No. 03 at 500 ml, its only size.

## Where the content comes from

The information is the old labels' information, only presented differently. Nothing has been added that those
labels don't carry.

- **Story:** the corrected history line, with a closing line per product taken from the old label's copy.
- **Facts row:**
  - species and origin, as in the old copy;
  - "best for", taken from each old label's copy;
  - "100% natural", from the old badge.
- **Front:** "Premium quality", from the old band.
- **Nutrition:** the old panel, the same on all three.
  - The old panel has a calories line with no figure in it, so the row is left out.
  - Add a calories figure only if production supplies one.
- **Marks:** USDA Organic, SGS and the EU organic leaf, taken from the old label file and used at a similar size.
- **Price, barcode, dates and maker:** as printed on the old labels.
  - MRP: 20,000 / 17,000 / 12,500.
  - Barcodes: …0007 / …0038 / …0083.
  - MFD 01/02/2025, EXP 31/01/2027.
  - Agroventuresexports, with its full address and contacts.

Taken out because the old labels don't carry them:
- ingredients and storage;
- alcohol strength;
- dose;
- the calculated energy figures.

## Marked in `PROOF-all-sizes.pdf`
